“Every detail of your space is considered with purpose — from the materials selected to the way light, proportion and functionality come together.”

Thank you for choosing Zen Interior. This document brings together everything that defines your project — the commercial summary, the scope we have agreed, the materials we have selected and the drawings that will guide our craftspeople on site.

We have prepared it to be read at leisure and kept for reference. As the project moves from design to handover, each revision of this document will record what has been decided, so that nothing about your home is left to memory.

Please review each section carefully. Where you would like to refine a detail, note it on the Client Notes page and your design lead will respond within two working days.

We look forward to building a home that feels calm, considered and entirely your own.
