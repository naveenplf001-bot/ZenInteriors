concept: |
  A working couple, a compact plan and a strong preference for surfaces that
  stay quiet. The apartment had no room for anything decorative, so the interest
  had to come from proportion and from how the materials meet.

  We reduced the palette to two: a soft grey lacquer and a warm walnut, set
  against stone. The lacquer runs full height wherever storage is needed and
  disappears; the walnut appears only where you touch the room, at the console,
  the drawer fronts and the shelf edges.

  A single handleless shadow line carries through the entrance, the kitchen and
  the living storage, so the whole apartment reads as one continuous piece
  rather than a set of separate installations.
execution: |
  Delivered in sixteen weeks. Every lacquer panel was sprayed and cured off site
  under controlled conditions, because a hand-finished lacquer in a live
  apartment will not hold its surface.

  The curved end returns on the kitchen and the entrance run were formed rather
  than mitred, which took longer at the workshop and removed every visible joint
  from the finished elevation.
materials:
  - Soft grey lacquer, sprayed and cured off site, in a low-sheen finish
  - Walnut veneer to console, drawer fronts and shelf edges
  - Large-format grey marble flooring with a continuous vein direction
  - Fluted black panelling as a single accent at the entrance
  - Integrated undercabinet and reveal lighting throughout
highlights:

  - Formed curved returns with no visible joint on the finished elevation
  - Lacquer sprayed and cured off site for a surface that holds
  - Sixteen-week delivery in an occupied building
