concept: |
  A workplace for a practice that brings clients into the office almost daily.
  The space had to work as a studio and read as a portfolio at the same time.

  Rather than dividing the floor into rooms, we ran one continuous oak surface
  the length of the plan and let the programme sit on it. Meeting, making and
  reviewing all happen along the same line, which is how the studio actually
  works.

  A material wall at the entrance holds full-size samples of everything the
  practice specifies. It is functional storage and it is the first thing a
  client sees.
execution: |
  Delivered in fourteen weeks around a live occupancy, with the studio moving
  between two halves of the floor as work progressed.

  Services were coordinated into the exposed ceiling before any joinery was set,
  so the finished soffit reads as one plane rather than a patchwork of
  penetrations.
materials:
  - European oak worktops in continuous nineteen-metre runs
  - Exposed structural soffit with fully coordinated services
  - Acoustic felt panelling in undyed wool
  - Blackened steel shelving and material display framing
